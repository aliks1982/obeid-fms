Connecting to Google Sheets...
📋 Two-step setup to connect your Google Sheet
Step 1 — Make sheet public (if not done)
1. Open your Google Sheet
2. Click Share (top right)
3. Click Change to anyone with the link
4. Set permission to Viewer
5. Click Done
Step 2 — Deploy Apps Script (solves CORS)
1. In your Sheet: Extensions → Apps Script
2. Paste the script shown below → Save
3. Click Deploy → New deployment → Web App
4. Set Execute as: Me · Anyone can access
5. Copy the URL, paste below, click Connect
